# Cast & performers

Every name you put in a video's **Cast** becomes a performer with their own page: their videos, photo, aliases and attributes.

## Finding performers

- **Click a name** on a video card to open that performer's page. Rest the pointer on a name to see their photo.
- **👥 Cast** in the top bar opens **Browse Cast Members**, a list of everyone:
  - **Search cast...** finds a name, an alias or an attribute. `and` / `or` combine words: `blonde and petite`.
  - **Sort** by number of videos or by name; **View** as a list or a grid with photos.
  - **Filter** by favorites, gender, category (Pro / Amateur / Celeb), external database link, how complete their attributes are, and whether they have a photo. With the ♂ filter you can also choose men in straight scenes (Male Cast) or in gay / bi / trans scenes.

## A performer's page

- **Photo**: **Set Photo** to upload one or give its web address (it's downloaded and kept in lustr), then **✂ Crop**. Click the photo to see it full size. You can also make any picture in the Images tab a performer's photo.
- **♥** marks a favorite. **Gender** and **Category** (Pro, Amateur, Celeb) are set here too.
- **Also known as**: aliases and other spellings. Their page also shows videos filed under an alias. Add one with **Add alias / alternate spelling…**, click a name to make it the main name, or **×** to remove it.
- **Appearance**: their attributes (see below).
- **Total Videos**, **Libraries** and **Library Distribution**: click a library to see only their videos there.
- **Search Videos**: shows their videos in **All Videos**, where all the sidebar filters work.
- **🎞 Clips** made from their videos, and **Photo albums** they're in.
- **Ext DB**: link them to StashDB or ThePornDB to bring in a photo, aliases and attributes.

Their videos are listed at the bottom. Select and right-click them as anywhere else.

## Attributes

A performer's **attributes** describe them: ethnicity, hair, body, and so on. Open **Edit Cast Attributes** on their page.

- Each value is **⭐ primary** or **☆ regular**. Primary is their stable or most common trait. Regular is one that only sometimes applies, like a hair colour they had for a while. Click the star to switch.
- Fields like ethnicity and face should have one primary value. Fields like hair and body can have several when they really coexist.
- The editor suggests values found on their videos. Tick the ones you want.
- While you type a value, a list suggests the values your videos already use in that field. **Enter** or **Tab** puts one in; **Esc** closes the list.

To put the primary attributes onto videos, select the videos and right-click → **Apply Cast Attributes**. Each performer's primary values are added to the videos they're in. Nothing is removed.

## Two performers with the same name

Sometimes two different people share a name. lustr numbers them: **Jane Doe (1)**, **Jane Doe (2)**.

- On the performer's page, **Split off videos…** moves the videos that are really someone else to a new, numbered performer.
- **Same name as:** lists the others, with their video counts and main site. **Make this the main one** makes this one number 1.
- When you type a shared name in Edit Metadata, lustr asks **which one** you mean.
- Searching `cast:"Jane Doe"` finds all of them.

## Male Cast

Men in straight scenes go in the video's **Male Cast** field (see [Editing metadata](metadata.md#male-cast)). They are full performers with their own pages, but are listed after the women, and the cast browser's ♂ filter can show them separately.

## Tidying up

**Settings → Maintenance** finds performers with no videos left, and the same performer spelled in different ways, so you can remove or merge them.
