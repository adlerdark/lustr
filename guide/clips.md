# Clips

A **clip** is a short GIF or video cut from one of your videos: a favourite moment to keep or share. lustr makes clips on the server, in the background, and keeps them with your library.

## Making a clip

1. Play the video and find the moment. To pick an exact stretch, set an A-B loop first: press **L** at the start, then **L** at the end.
2. Click **✂ Clip** in the player's top bar. (With a loop set it reads **✂ Clip the loop**.)
3. In **✂ Make a clip**:
   - Give it a **Title**.
   - Adjust the start and end with **-1s**, **-.1**, **+.1**, **+1s**, or **now** for the player's current time. **▶ Preview** loops that stretch in the player.
   - Choose the format: **WebM** (small and sharp), **MP4** (plays everywhere) or **GIF** (no sound, big files, so keep it short). Then the **Size (short side)**, **Frame rate**, **Shape** (**Original**, **Square 1:1** or **Vertical 9:16**, with where to crop), **Quality** (**Standard** or **High (bigger)**), and **Sound** (not for GIFs).
4. Click **Make clip**. The player says **Clip queued**, and you can keep watching.

## Your clips

**🎞 Clips** at the bottom of the sidebar opens all of them. A performer's page has a **🎞 Clips** button for theirs, and **File Info** shows a video's own clips.

- Filter by format, library or title, and sort by **Newest** or **Oldest**.
- Click a clip to play it full size; **←** / **→** step through them.
- Each clip can be **downloaded**, its link copied, opened in a new tab, **renamed** or **deleted**.
- **▶ source** plays the original video from the clip's start.

> Note: clips are files lustr makes and keeps in its data folder. Your original videos are never changed.
