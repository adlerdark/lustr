# Images

lustr also shows the pictures in your library folders. Each library has an **Images** tab. The pictures are grouped into **albums**, one for each folder. A `.zip` file counts as a folder too; nothing is unpacked.

## Albums

- **Find an album, cast, tag…** searches album names and their details.
- Sort by **Title**, **Cast**, **Most photos** or **Newest**, and tick **Favorites first**.
- **♥** on an album marks it as a favorite.
- Click an album to open it; **‹ Albums** (or **Esc**) goes back.

## Album details

An album can have what a video has: a title, cast, site, tags and the other fields, plus the **videos** it goes with (say, the photo set from the same shoot). Click **Edit details…** in an open album, or select several albums (**Ctrl+click** / **Shift+click**) and click **Edit details…** to edit them together. As in Edit Metadata, a list suggests existing values as you type.

The album's performers appear on it. Click one to open their page, which in turn lists their **Photo albums**. The videos you linked appear under **Videos** in the album.

## The viewer

Click a photo to view it.

| Key / button | Does |
|---|---|
| **←** / **→** | Previous / next photo |
| **Space** | Slideshow (or **▶ Slideshow** in the album) |
| **+** / **-** / **0** | Zoom in / out / back. Ctrl+wheel and double-click zoom too. |
| **F** | Full screen |
| **S** | Favorite |
| **Esc** | Close |

The buttons in the viewer also make a photo the album's **cover**, or a **performer's photo** (you then crop it), and open the original file.

## Short videos as images

Short loops and GIF-like videos often belong with pictures rather than with your videos. Right-click a video → **🖼 Show in Images instead** to move it to the Images tab. There it plays on a loop, with sound, like a GIF. A library whose type is images-only shows everything this way.

## Skipping folders

Many videos come with a folder of screenshots. To keep those out of the Images tab, list the folder names in **Settings → Browsing → Images tab: folders to skip** (comma-separated; any depth, any case).
