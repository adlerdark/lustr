# Collections & playlists

lustr has two ways to keep videos together:

- A **collection** is a set of videos you keep, like a shelf. It can be one you fill by hand, or a **smart** one that fills itself from filters.
- A **playlist** is a list you watch in order, one video after the next.

## Collections

Collections are on each library's **Collections** tab. In **All Videos** you see every collection; in a library or group, the ones that belong to it.

### A collection you fill by hand

1. On the **Collections** tab, click **+ New collection** and give it a name.
2. Select videos anywhere, right-click → **Add to collection…**, and pick it.

Inside a collection, right-click a video → **Remove from** *(the collection)* to take it out, or **Use as collection cover** to make it the collection's picture.

### A smart collection

A smart collection is a saved view: its filters, search and sort. It keeps itself up to date, so new videos that fit appear in it by themselves.

1. On the **Library** tab, set up what you want to see: the search, tags, sites, length, resolution, sort.
2. Click **★ Save as smart collection** and give it a name.

Smart collections are marked **smart**. To change what one shows, set the filters on the Library tab, then open the collection and click **Use current Library filters**.

### Options

Open a collection to change it:

- **Edit**: its name and description. **Delete** removes the collection, not its videos.
- The sort menu (hand-filled collections): **Order added**, **Title**, **Recently added**, **Most played**, **Last played** or **Time watched**. A smart collection uses the sort it was saved with.
- **Show on Home**: the collection becomes a row on Home and on the library's **Recommended** tab.

## Playlists

**📋 Playlists** in the top bar opens the playlists. **+ New** makes one. The **Rename** and **Delete** buttons next to each playlist do what they say.

- To add videos, select them and right-click → **Add to Playlist...**.
- **Add to Watchlist** puts a video on the built-in **Watchlist**, a playlist for "watch this later".
- Inside a playlist, right-click → **Remove from** *(the playlist)* takes a video out.

Playing a video from a playlist adds **◀ Prev** and **Next ▶** to the player (or press **P** / **N**). With **auto-next** ticked, the next video starts when one ends. See [Watching videos](player.md#playlists).

**Back to Library** returns to your videos.
