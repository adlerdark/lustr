# Getting started

This page takes you from a fresh install to watching your first video. Installing lustr itself (Docker, the data folder, the video folders) is covered in the [README](https://github.com/adlerdark/lustr#install).

## 1. Create your account

The first time you open lustr it shows **Create Account**. The first account becomes the owner; the password needs at least 8 characters. Do this before anyone else can reach the page.

> Note: lustr logs you out after 15 minutes without activity, and when you close the browser. You can change the timeout in **Settings → Account & Security**.

## 2. Make a library

A **library** is a set of folders whose videos you browse together, for example one for each kind of video you keep.

1. Open **Settings** (⚙ at the top right) → **Libraries & Scans**.
2. Type a name in **New Library Name...** and click **Create Library**.
3. Click **+ Folder** on the new library. A folder browser opens on the folders lustr can see (`/media`, and `/media0` if you mounted a second one).
4. Click a folder to open it, tick the boxes of the folders you want, then **Add Selected**. Or, while inside a folder, **Add Current**.

lustr scans the new folders right away. Videos appear within seconds; their length, codec and resolution fill in shortly after.

> Note: lustr never changes, moves or deletes your video files. Your video folders are mounted read-only.

You can also add folders later from the library itself: hover it in the left sidebar, click its gear icon (**Manage Library**), then **+ Add Folder**.

## 3. Groups (optional)

A **group** gathers libraries into one collapsible entry in the sidebar. Click the group name to see the videos of all its libraries together. Groups can hold other groups. Make one under **Settings → Libraries & Scans → Groups**: type a name in **New Group Name...**, click **Create Group**, then **Edit** it to choose what it contains.

## 4. Thumbnails

New videos start without a picture. To make thumbnails for the library you're in, click 📷 in the top bar, or use **Manage Library → Generate Missing Thumbs**. It works in the background; you can keep browsing.

Later you can pick a better frame while watching: press **T** in the player to make the current frame the thumbnail. See [Watching videos](player.md#keyboard-shortcuts).

## 5. A tour of the screen

**Left sidebar**

- **Libraries**: *Home*, your libraries and groups, and *All Videos*. A ⚠️ next to a library means files were added, moved or removed outside lustr; click it to review the changes.
- **Length**: show only videos of a certain length.
- **Resolution**: show only 4K, HD and up, and so on.
- **Tags / Sites**: click a tag to show only videos with it, click again to hide videos with it, and a third time to clear it. Right-click hides it straight away.

**Top bar**

- The **search box**. See [Browsing & search](browsing.md#searching).
- **Sort**, ★ **favorites only**, **Select All**, and the view buttons (thumbnails, list, folders).
- 👥 **Cast** for your performers, and 🔍 **Metadata** to browse every value of every field.
- ⚙ **Settings**.
- The **☰ menu** at the far right: **Help** (this guide), **Link a TV**, **Check for Updates** and **Log Out**. A green dot on it means a new version is out.
- **Rescan** looks for new, moved and deleted files in the current library.

At the bottom of the sidebar are **Manage Libraries**, **🎞 Clips**, and the version you're running. When a newer version is out, an **Update available** notice appears there instead.

You can hide the top bar buttons you don't use in **Settings → Appearance**.

**The videos**

- **Click** a video to select it, and **double-click** to play it.
- **Ctrl+click** adds or removes a video from the selection; **Shift+click** selects a range.
- **Right-click** opens the video's menu: edit metadata, add to a playlist, mark as favorite, and more.

On a phone, ☰ at the top left opens the sidebar.

## 6. Play something

Double-click any video. The player opens full-screen; **Esc** closes it. Everything about the player is on [Watching videos](player.md).
