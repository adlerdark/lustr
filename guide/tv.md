# Link a TV

A TV app for lustr can log in with a short code, so you never have to type your password with a remote.

## Linking

1. Open the lustr app on the TV. It shows a **4-digit code**.
2. In lustr in your browser, open **Settings → Account & Security → Link a TV**. Or click 📺 in the top bar, which takes you there.
3. Type the code and click **Link**. The TV logs in within a few seconds.

A code is valid for 5 minutes. If it runs out, or lustr restarts in the meantime, the TV shows a new one.

## How long a TV stays logged in

At most **4 hours**. The inactivity timeout (**Log out after inactivity**, on the same page) applies to TVs too.

## Logging TVs out

**Logged-in TVs**, under Link a TV, lists every TV that's logged in. **Log out** ends one; **Log out all TVs** ends them all. Changing your password also logs out every other device.

> Note: only you, logged in, can approve a code. Wrong codes are rate-limited, so a code can't be guessed.
