# Notes

**📝 Notes** in the top bar opens a notepad: a place for to-do lists, names to look up, or anything else you want to keep next to your library.

- It **saves as you type**. There's no Save button.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes, and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** or **Ctrl+Y** redoes.
- The notes are kept on the server, so you see the same text on every device.

If you don't use it, hide the button in **Settings → Appearance**.
